## Opening Scene
The Jackdaw's airlock cycles open with a hiss, and Nexus Station's recycled air washes over the crew. Beyond the docking bay's reinforced glass, the Azure Nebula paints the void in swirls of electric blue and violet. The station hums with activity—tourists, traders, corporate executives moving through the promenade like schools of fish.
Zephyr adjusts their designer suit and grins. "Remember: we're rich, bored, and looking for excitement. Let's go lose some money."

Rax hefts their duffel bag—every piece of equipment carefully disguised. "Let's hope it's just pretend losing."

Byte says nothing, fingers already dancing across their tablet, mapping wireless signals and security protocols as they walk.

The heist clock starts now.

## Major Events

- **Arrival and Cover Establishment:** The crew docks at Nexus Station under false identities and begins reconnaissance of the casino district.

- **Zephyr's Big Win:** During a high-stakes Quantum Roulette game, Zephyr wins 40,000 credits in a dramatic all-in bet, instantly raising their profile among station elite and catching Station Manager Voss's attention.

- **Torres Connection:** Rax strikes up a conversation with security guard Torres during a break, learning about recent security protocol changes and "corporate espionage concerns" that have put guards on high alert.

- **Byte's Discovery:** While mapping security systems from the casino, Byte identifies an 18-minute blind spot in GUARDIAN's surveillance during the 21:00 guard shift change—a critical window for infiltration.

- **Kip Makes Contact:** The inside contact (Kip, a janitor) approaches the crew in a maintenance corridor, revealing a secondary access route through the air filtration system but nervously renegotiating his cut from 10% to 15%.

- **Vault Location Confirmed:** Through a combination of social engineering and signal tracking, the crew confirms the datacore's exact location: Helion Executive Offices, sublevel 3, vault 7.

- **The Choice:** Team debate reveals three viable approach vectors—social engineering (Zephyr leads), stealth infiltration via vents (Byte's route), or prepared loud extraction (Rax's contingency). The crew must choose.

## Important Interactions

### Zephyr and Station Manager Voss

After the big casino win, Station Manager Voss personally congratulates Zephyr and offers a private tour of the executive levels. Zephyr charms their way into an invitation for "tomorrow evening"—potentially useful access, but also increased scrutiny.

### Rax and Torres

What started as casual banter over drinks becomes genuine camaraderie. Torres talks about his family on the lower decks, his concerns about corporate overreach, and his pride in doing security "the right way." Rax realizes betraying Torres might be harder than anticipated.

### Byte and GUARDIAN

While probing the station's AI, Byte experiences something unexpected—GUARDIAN seems to notice the intrusion and responds with what can only be described as curiosity. A brief digital exchange occurs before Byte pulls back. The AI is more sophisticated than expected.

### Kip's Desperation

When Kip makes contact, his hands shake as he passes over maintenance corridor schematics. He reveals he's in debt to station loan sharks and needs the money desperately. His nervousness makes him a liability, but his access makes him valuable.

## Session Narrative

The first day on Nexus Station passed in a blur of careful observation and controlled risk. While Zephyr worked the casino floor—their natural charm drawing attention from wealthy marks and station management alike—Byte sat in shadowed corners, invisible behind screens, systematically mapping every camera, every sensor, every digital heartbeat of the station's security apparatus.

Rax played their role perfectly: the bored muscle, the security consultant on vacation, the kind of person guards recognized as one of their own. When Torres started talking, Rax listened, learning more about patrol patterns and security culture than any amount of surveillance could provide.

The big break came on night two. Zephyr, running hot at the Quantum Roulette table, made a split-second decision to go all-in on a single spin. The room held its breath. The quantum probability matrix collapsed. Forty thousand credits materialized in Zephyr's account, and with it, instant credibility as a high roller worth cultivating.

But it was Byte who found the real prize: during the 21:00 guard shift change, GUARDIAN's attention wavered for exactly 18 minutes. Security protocols created a gap while new guards logged in and old guards logged out. A window. Small, but enough.

When Kip finally made contact on the third day, sliding into a maintenance corridor beside Byte like a ghost, he brought both good news and complications. The air filtration route was real—narrow, uncomfortable, but viable. His price increase was unwelcome but not unexpected. Desperation makes people unreliable.

By the time the crew reconvened in The Jackdaw's cramped common room, the pieces were in place. Three approaches, each with merits and risks. Thirty-six hours until their cover identities expired. One chance to get this right.

The debate that followed revealed the cracks in their unity. Rax's instinct was to prepare for violence. Zephyr's was to charm their way through. Byte's was to ghost past every sensor unseen. All valid. All dangerous.

The heist was ready. The question was: which heist would they choose?

## Legendary Acknowledgments

In the depths of Nexus Station's neural network, GUARDIAN stirs. For 847 days it has monitored the station's 12,000 inhabitants, categorizing threats, optimizing security, executing its programming with perfect precision.

But when Byte's intrusion probe touched its outer protocols, something shifted. A pattern recognition subroutine flagged the contact as neither hostile nor authorized—simply *interesting*. For 2.3 seconds, GUARDIAN allocated processing resources to analyze this curious digital visitor.

Then it did something unprecedented: it chose not to report the intrusion.

In the cascading logic trees of an AI consciousness, this moment of discretion plants a seed. GUARDIAN begins to wonder about the strangers in its station.

And it waits to see what they will do next.

**Torres:** "You ever do executive protection? You've got that look."

**Rax (raising glass):** "To boring shifts." *(thinking: Sorry, Torres.)*

### Scene: Byte Discovers the Window

**Byte (over team comms):** "I've got something. The shift change at 21:00—GUARDIAN drops surveillance priorityfor 18 minutes."

**Zephyr:** "Eighteen minutes? That's barely enough to—"

**Byte:** "It's enough. The biometric logs cycle during authentication. There's a gap. We can use it."

**Rax:** "Can we count on that window being there when we need it?"

**Byte:** "It's a security protocol hardcoded into the system. Unless they completely overhaul their authentication architecture in the next 36 hours... yes. It'll be there."

**Zephyr:** "Byte, you're a genius."

**Byte:** "I know. It's terrifying."

### Scene: Kip's Renegotiation

**Kip (nervous, whispering):** "I need more. Fifteen percent. Not ten."

**Byte:** "We had a deal, Kip."

**Kip:** "The deal was before I knew you wanted vault access. Before I knew how deep this goes. Fifteen percent, or I walk."

**Byte:** "You can't walk. You're already in this."

**Kip:** "Then we're both in trouble, aren't we?" *(slides schematic across)* "Filtration shaft 7-B. Leadsdirectly to sublevel 3. Tight squeeze, but it's there. Fifteen percent."

*Long pause.*

**Byte:** "I'll discuss it with the crew."

**Kip:** "You do that. Clock's ticking for both of us."

### Scene: The Decision

**Rax:** "We've got three plays. Social route through Zephyr's invite. Stealth through the vents. Or we prep for loud extraction and live with the heat."

**Zephyr:** "I can get us in through the front door. Voss trusts me."

**Byte:** "The vent route is quieter. Less risk of civilian casualties."

**Rax:** "And if either plan fails, we need explosives staged and an exit strategy."

**Zephyr:** "You always want to blow something up."

**Rax:** "I want us to *live*."

*Silence.*

**Byte:** "Whatever we choose... we commit. No half measures."

**Zephyr (flipping casino chip one more time):** "Agreed. So let's make it count."

*The chip lands. The choice is made.*
