# Session Log: The Uneasy Truce at Bramblebend

## 🎬 Opening Scene

The session began in the immediate aftermath of the bridge skirmish. The party and the remnants of Captain Vorn's guards were licking their wounds in the ruins near the riverbank. Vorn, realizing his situation was hopeless against the party and that his support was gone, hesitantly approached the party's makeshift camp, carrying his own white surrender flag (a ripped shirt).

## 🧭 Major Events

- **The Parley:** The party (specifically Ria) met with Captain Vorn, establishing a fragile truce. Vorn was immediately cooperative but emphasized that his orders came directly from the Baron and that defying them carried a death penalty.
- **Ria’s Fatigue and Consequence:** Ria attempted to use her last healing item on a civilian but failed the check due to extreme stress, resulting in the loss of her last **Consumables: Bandages (0)** and the immediate increase of her **Stress Level: High (3/5)**.
- **Baron's Messenger:** A rider was spotted heading toward the bridge from the south. Vorn confirmed this was the Baron's scout, setting the **two-day timer** before the main military force arrives.
- **Intel Exchange:** In exchange for the party sparing his life, Vorn revealed the Baron's true motive: securing an ancient artifact, not just tolls. He hinted that the **Missing Map** (leading to the artifact) was hidden in the ruined gatehouse of the bridge.

## 🤝 Important Interactions

- **Ria and Vorn:** Their relationship shifted from adversarial to an **Uneasy Associate** status. Vorn is calculating; Ria is deeply fatigued by the political maneuvering required.
- **The Townsfolk:** The party's attempts to help the remaining townsfolk were rebuffed; the people of Bramblebend are terrified of the Baron's response and see the party as a liability. This directly contributes to the town's **fearful** status.

## 📖 Session Narrative

The atmosphere was heavy with moral fatigue and political danger. The session was less about combat and more about negotiation under duress. The party gained a temporary truce and valuable intelligence (the map), but at the cost of Ria's well-being and placing the entire town of Bramblebend in immediate danger from the Baron’s approaching troops.

## 🖼️ Closing Image

Captain Vorn returned to his men, still guarding the broken bridge but now reporting to the party. Ria, slumped against a ruined wall, stared blankly at the map Vorn had drawn on a scrap of paper—a map showing the escape routes from the region. The choice for the next session was clear: **flee or fight the Baron's army.**
