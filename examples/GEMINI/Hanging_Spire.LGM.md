## 🕵️ Hidden Truths
- **The Vacuum Mechanism:** The Spire's foundation is made of "Null-Stone," which consumes the history of any Shard within a 50-mile radius. The closer the party gets to their goal, the more the Spire (and Valerius) will thrive.
- **The Ghost in the Orrery:** There is a sentient AI/Spirit trapped in the stopped gears of the Great Orrery. It is trying to communicate through the weeping statues in the courtyard.

The Spire isn't just a library; it is a **Vacuum**. It is actively "sucking" memories from the surrounding Shards to keep its own lights on. The Archive-Knights are unknowingly contributing to the world's collapse by bringing Memory-Cores here.

## ⚠️ Future Triggers
- If a player succeeds on a **History Check (DC 20)** in the Orrery, they notice the gears aren't broken—they've been intentionally locked.
- If the party loses their "Soul-Gem," they must roll on the **Amnesia Table** every 10 minutes of play.

- **Trigger - The First Fill:** When the party records their first "Stable Memory" into the Soul-Gem, the Spire's lights will brighten significantly. This is a clue that the Spire is feeding.
- **Trigger - The Weeping Mercury:** If the party returns the mercury from the statues to the Orrery, the "Ghost" will reveal the location of the *true* Archive.
- **Trigger - Total Amnesia:** If the party fails a "Stability Check" while holding the Gem, they lose access to their `CS.md` traits for one scene.
