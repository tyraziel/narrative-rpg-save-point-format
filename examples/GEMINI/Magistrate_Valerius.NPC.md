## 🕸️ Secret Motives
Valerius has already "sold" the party's memories. He has a secret pact with the Void-Cultists; in exchange for the "Soul-Gem" the party is carrying (which is actually a trap), the Cultists will keep the Hanging Spire afloat for another decade. He sent Kaelen specifically because he knows Kaelen’s family history makes him easy to manipulate.

## 🎒 Secret Inventory
- **The Obsidian Key:** Can remotely "vibrate" the Soul-Gem the party carries, causing them to suffer memory loss at a distance.
- **Tome of the Unwritten:** A book that physically erases its own text every night; Valerius uses it to hide his ledger of "sacrificed" Shards.

## 🤝 Secret Relationships
- **The Void-Cult (The Unremembered):** Co-conspirators.
- **Kaelen Vane:** Valerius actually knows Kaelen is innocent of his past "disgrace," but keeps the truth hidden to maintain leverage.
