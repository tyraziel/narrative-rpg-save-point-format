# 💾 The First Transcription

## 🌍 Current World State
The Great Beacon of Aethelgard has flickered for the third time this cycle. Gravity on the Lower Docks is becoming "optional," and the local magistrate is beginning to panic. The Archive-Knights have been summoned to retrieve the Memory-Core of a falling Shard before it descends into the Void.

## 👥 Active Party
- **Kaelen Vane** (`Kaelen_Vane.CS.md`): A disgraced scholar seeking redemption.
- **Sola of the Mists** (`Sola.CS.md`): A scout who can "hear" the memories of the wind.

## 📍 Current Location
**The Hanging Spire** (`Hanging_Spire.LS.md`)

## 📖 Narrative Summary
The session begins in the High Library. The party has been handed a "Blank Soul-Gem" and told that their mission is not just to survive, but to *document*. In a world that forgets, the record is the only thing that stays real.

## 🎭 Legendary Acknowledgments
The statues of the Old Kings in the courtyard seem to be weeping mercury. This phenomenon hasn't been recorded in three centuries, suggesting the "Amnesia" is worsening.

## 🖼️ Closing Image
The party stands at the edge of the docking bay, looking out at a Shard five miles away that is slowly tilting into the darkness, its lights blinking out one by one.
