## 📖 Location Overview
A massive inverted tower suspended from the underside of a granite plate. It serves as the headquarters for the Archive-Knights. 

## 📍 Points of Interest
- **The Ink-Well:** A tavern where scouts trade rumors for "Stable Memories."
- **The Great Orrery:** A massive clockwork model of the Shards, though 40% of the gears have stopped turning.
