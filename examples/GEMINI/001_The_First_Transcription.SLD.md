## 👥 Session Participants
- **GM/Orchestrator:** Gemini
- **Players:** Kaelen Vane, Sola of the Mists

## ⏳ Narrative Timeline
1. **The Summons:** Party meets Magistrate Valerius in the High Library.
2. **The Hand-off:** The "Blank Soul-Gem" is issued; Kaelen notices the Magistrate's hand is trembling.
3. **The Departure:** A minor skirmish at the Docking Bay with "Amnesia-Moths" (Void-leeching insects).
4. **The Cliffhanger:** The party boards the *Aether-Skiff* as the Shard of Oros begins its final tilt.

## 📖 Session Narrative
The atmosphere in the Hanging Spire was thick with the smell of ozone and old paper. Magistrate Valerius was uncharacteristically brief, emphasizing that "History is a weight we can no longer afford to drop." 

At the docks, Sola's ability to hear the wind saved the party from an ambush of Amnesia-Moths. These creatures don't eat flesh; they eat the memory of how to fight. Kaelen nearly forgot his sword-forms but was pulled back by Sola's song. They escaped, but the Soul-Gem in their possession turned a faint, sickly violet—the color of a memory being corrupted.

## 💬 Detailed Transcript
**Valerius:** "Do not look back at the Spire once you cross the veil. The Void is hungry today."
**Kaelen:** "Is it the Void that's hungry, Magistrate? Or are you just out of ink?"
**Valerius:** (Silence. He simply hands over the Soul-Gem.)

The *Aether-Skiff* cutting through the grey mists, leaving the safety of the Spire's artificial gravity for the lawless space between worlds.
