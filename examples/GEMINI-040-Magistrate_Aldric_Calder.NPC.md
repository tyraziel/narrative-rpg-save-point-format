## Secret Motives

Aldric is not merely a patron of the party; he is using them as "Resonance Probes." He is aware that the party's unique tactical synergy (and Korrin's overclocked harness) generates specific harmonic frequencies during Torse matches. 

His true goal is to use the Torse semifinals to mask a **Controlled Core Venting**. The "pressure spikes" the party detected are not accidents; Aldric is intentionally over-pressurizing the Gearworks to "bloom" an ancient resonance artifact beneath the arena. He needs the party to win not for House Calder's glory, but because their presence on the field is the catalyst for the artifact's activation.

If the sabotage is exposed, he is prepared to frame the party as the culprits, using the "irregular pressure readings" Elara detected as "evidence" of their tampering.

## Secret Inventory

- **Master Gear-Key (Sublevel C):** Provides unrestricted access to the Core Pressure Engine.
- **Encrypted Ledger:** Details unauthorized diversions of steam-power from the residential tiers to the Arena Sublevels.
- **The "Fail-Safe" Signal:** A small, brass pocket-device that, when triggered, will cause Korrin's pneumatic harness to seize, effectively "pausing" the match if the party gets too close to discovering the truth.
- **Letter from the Iron Syndicate:** Proof that Aldric is being blackmailed by a rival faction to facilitate the core venting.

## Secret Relationships

- **Ressa Quill (The Rival):** Aldric is her true sponsor. He has promised her a seat on the Council if she ensures the party remains "distracted" on the arena floor during the pressure surge.
- **The Junior Engineer:** This is not a "quiet confirmation" of help; the engineer is Aldric's informant, planted to feed Elara just enough "hidden" info to keep her looking in the wrong direction.
- **The Iron Syndicate:** A debt-based alliance. They hold the "Calder Succession Papers," which prove Aldric's lineage is fraudulent.
